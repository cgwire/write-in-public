# The Studio Life

**A blog about running an animation studio, written in public, one article at a time.**

Hundreds of articles. 10 acts. Published in order, roughly 4 weeks per act. Co-authored with the industry.

📍 **[The map](/map)** — every article, live status, and what's open · 💬 **[Discord](#)** · ✉️ **[Newsletter](#)** · 🛠 **[CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)**

## Why this exists

There are good blogs and books about animation as a craft, and good books about business in general. There is almost nothing about the part in between: how the money actually reaches a studio, what a pipeline costs before it earns anything, when to hire a coordinator instead of another artist, what a studio is worth on the day its founder wants out.

That knowledge exists. It's in the heads of a few thousand people, and it's transmitted at festival bars, in DMs, and to whoever happened to be in the room. 

*The Studio Life* is an attempt to write it down. In the open, with names on it, across 2D series, 3D service work, VFX, motion graphics and game visuals, in more than one territory. Not one studio's opinion published as doctrine, but the actual shape of each decision and who each answer suits.

## How this is made

- **Everything is versioned.** Articles ship at 1.0 and stay open. Corrections, new territories and reversed conclusions land as new versions with a visible changelog and a named reviser. Anything covering tooling, AI, real-time or funding programmes gets re-checked every six months.
- **Everything is credited.** Authors, section writers, reviewers, interviewees, and the person who fixed one wrong number are all named — in the frontmatter, on the page, and in the compiled PDF. Interviewees choose their own attribution, including anonymous, after they see how they've been used.
- **Nothing is finished in private.** Drafts open as pull requests at T-8, 4 weeks of lead time on every article, and a public community round at T-4 where practitioners get to say "that's not how it works in Tokyo" before it publishes rather than after.

Full detail on all of it — the timeline, the frontmatter, house style, sourcing and anonymity rules, licensing — is in **[CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)**.

## Contributing

Most of what will make this book good won't arrive as an article. It'll arrive as a corrected figure, a war story, a bid teardown, or forty-five minutes on a call.

Ways in, from smallest to largest: a **correction** via *Suggest an edit* on any published article · **testimony** or **data**, named or anonymous · an **interview** · a **section** of someone else's article · a whole **article**, claimed from the map · shaping a **season outline** during its dark week.

Nothing here requires a GitHub account. If issues and PRs aren't your tooling, say so in Discord or by email and someone will carry it into the repo for you, with credit.
